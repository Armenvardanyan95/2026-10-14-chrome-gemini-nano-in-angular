Chrome now ships a real AI model right inside the browser. No API keys, no servers, no per-token bills!

This Wednesday, join me as we build an Angular app on top of Gemini Nano, Chrome's built-in model: we'll turn receipt photos and messy emails into typed Signal Forms with structured output, control the app with plain-English commands, and translate foreign receipts on the fly, all on-device. We'll also talk about where Nano falls short and when you still want a cloud model.
